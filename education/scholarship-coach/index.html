Subject: Recommendation Letter Request for [Scholarship Name]

Dear [Teacher/Counselor Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I am applying for [Scholarship Name] with a deadline of [Deadline Date], and I would be honored if you would write a letter of recommendation on my behalf.

Thank you so much for considering my request.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Interview Preparation

Common scholarship interview questions and tips

Tell us about yourself.

Tip: Keep it brief (2-3 minutes). Focus on your academic interests, key activities, and what makes you unique. End with why you're excited about your future.

Why do you deserve this scholarship?

Tip: Focus on alignment between the scholarship's mission and your goals. Mention specific achievements and how the scholarship will help you make an impact.

What are your career goals?

Tip: Be specific but realistic. Connect your major to future impact. Show you've thought about your path but remain open to growth.

Describe a challenge you've overcome.

Tip: Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Focus more on what you learned and how you grew than on the struggle itself.

How will you give back to your community?

Tip: Show genuine commitment to service. Reference past volunteer work or specific plans. Connect it to your field of study if possible.

What makes you different from other candidates?

Tip: Highlight unique experiences, perspectives, or combinations of interests. Be authentic—don't try to be someone you're not.

Do you have any questions for us?

Tip: Always have 1-2 thoughtful questions prepared. Ask about the scholarship's history, past recipients, or how you can stay connected with the organization.
